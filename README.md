# SELF BOT DISCORD — Version 1

[Español](README.es.md)

## Download

**[Download for Windows](https://github.com/CerditoCR/self-bot-discord/releases/latest/download/SELF-BOT-DISCORD.exe)**

Open `SELF-BOT-DISCORD.exe`. No Python installation is required.

## Use

1. Choose your language and theme.
2. Enter your account token and click **Check account**. Surrounding quotes are accepted.
3. Add your channel IDs, one at a time.
4. Write your message, optionally add an image, and set the interval.
5. Choose **Create BAT on Desktop** to run locally, or **Create ZIP for Discloud** to upload it to Discloud.

**Local:** open the desktop BAT. CMD displays logs. Press **Ctrl+C** or close CMD to stop. Keep the generated folder beside the BAT. The first round starts immediately.

**Edit a package:** click **Load BAT or ZIP**, make your changes, then click **Update loaded file**. Restart the BAT or upload the updated ZIP to Discloud.

Never share your token or generated packages. [Discord prohibits automation of personal accounts](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/115002192352-Automated-User-Accounts-Self-Bots).
